What the PC went through today and on earlier days, and what weighs on it right now.

Three groups of pages cover it: **Activity** (**Today** and **History**), **Now (live)** (**Live** and **Programs**) and **Freezes and stutters** (**All events**).

## Today

A sentence sums up the day, such as normal usage for most of the day with one freeze, and names the worst moment.

**PC states** is a timeline from midnight to now. Each stretch is **Normal**, **High usage**, **Overloaded** or **Suspended** (asleep), and each freeze is marked on it.

- Point at a moment to see what the CPU, the memory, the disk and the GPU were doing.
- Click a mark to open its report. Click a state to show only that state; click again to show all.
- **Ctrl** and the mouse wheel zoom; **0** shows the whole day.

Below the timeline, **Day events** lists the day's freezes and overload periods, and **Last 14 days** counts the freezes of each day. **See all events** and **See the history** lead further.

## Overload periods

An overload period is a stretch when the CPU, the memory or a disk stayed at its limit, such as memory nearly full for twelve minutes. Each one opens on its own page:

- the period, its peak and its average;
- a chart of the period with the minutes around it;
- **Programs that used the most** during it;
- **Freezes within the period**;
- **What to do**.

## History

**History** shows the freezes of earlier days on a calendar, by **Month**, **Week** or **2 weeks**. **Freezes by weekday** shows which days of the week are worst, and **Day by day** lists each day. Select a day to see its freezes and open their reports.

How far back it goes depends on **Settings › Data retention**.

## All events

**All events** lists today's occurrences and overload periods in a table, with their time, type, origin, duration and severity. Filter it by **All**, **Freezes**, **Stutters**, **Programs not responding** or **Overloads**.

## Live

**Live** shows what weighs on the PC at this moment:

- **Last 10 minutes**: CPU, memory, disk and GPU, updated every second. Vestigium keeps these ten minutes in memory, and the data before each freeze comes from here.
- **Programs using the most now**, each with all its processes added up.
- **Programs not responding**, when any is.
- **Last freeze**, with a link to its report.

Hold **Ctrl** to pause the screen on one second, as in Task Manager; release it to resume.

## Programs

**Programs** lists every program running. Sort by any column, or type in **Filter programs**. Open a program to see its file and where it lives, its signature, which user it runs as, its CPU and memory over the last 10 minutes, and the day's freezes it took part in.

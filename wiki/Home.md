Vestigium finds out why a Windows PC freezes, stutters or crashes, and what to do about it.

A small monitor runs in the background from the tray and keeps the last minutes of what the PC was doing. When the PC stops for a moment, stutters in a game or a call, or shuts down unexpectedly, Vestigium turns it into a report that names the likely cause and shows the evidence. The window also reads the crash dumps Windows keeps, checks the disks and the hardware, tests the memory and the disks, and suggests Windows settings to change, each one reversible.

Vestigium runs on Windows 10 22H2 and Windows 11 (x64), in English and Brazilian Portuguese. It monitors the one PC it is installed on, and what it keeps stays on that PC.

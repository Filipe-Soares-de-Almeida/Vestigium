Every setting, group by group. Open **Settings** from its card on Home, or type a setting's name in the search.

While Vestigium is not running, settings are read-only.

## Colors

- **Color mode**: **Like Windows** (until changed), **Light** or **Dark**.
- **Color theme**: **Red** (the default), **Graphite**, **Slate** or **Indigo**. The themes change only the window colours; the app icon stays red.

## Reports

- **Detail level** for every screen: **Simple** (summary and actions), **Detailed** (with measured values) or **Technical** (counters, events and IDs).

## Language

- **Vestigium's language**: **Like Windows**, **English** or **Portuguese (Brazil)**. The tray menu changes at once, the window the next time Vestigium opens.

## Monitoring

- **Start with Windows**: starts monitoring at sign-in, without administrator rights. Off until you turn it on.
- **Sensitive mode**: checks the PC every 16 ms instead of 100 ms, to catch short stutters. It turns itself off after 30 minutes.
- **Pause monitoring**: pick a **Pause length** of **15 min**, **1 h**, **4 h** or **Until restart**, then **Pause**. Nothing is measured during a pause, so a freeze in that time has no report. **Resume now** ends it early.

## Data retention

How long each kind of data is kept on this PC:

| Kind | Choices | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Per-second data** | 24 hours, 2 days, 3 days or 7 days | 2 days |
| **Per-minute summary** | 30, 90 or 180 days, or 1 year | 90 days |
| **Freezes and reports** | 90 or 180 days, 1 year or 2 years | 1 year |

**Expected space** shows how much disk the chosen periods take. Shorter periods take less.

## Updates

- **Check for updates automatically**: when the window opens, at most once a day, Vestigium checks GitHub for a newer version. No PC data is sent.
- **Installed version**, with **Check now**.
- **What's new**: the notes of every version.

See [Updating](./Updating.md).

## Privacy and data

- **Data kept**: the version, and the space the history takes.
- **Open the data folder** opens it in File Explorer.
- **Delete the history…**, then **Delete everything**, deletes the history after a confirmation. Settings stay.

See [Privacy and data](./Privacy-and-data.md).

How the window is organised, and how to move around in it.

## Opening and closing

Open the window with **Open Vestigium** in the tray menu, or with **Vestigium** in the Start menu. Opening it again brings the open window forward.

Closing the window closes only the window. Monitoring goes on from the tray.

## Home

The window opens on Home:

- At the top, the name and whether Vestigium is monitoring.
- Four cards with a one-line status: **Health**, **Activity**, **Optimizations** and **Settings**.
- A **Search** box: type a word such as disk, memory or freeze to jump to a page, a tweak or a setting.
- A tile for each group of pages.

| Group | What it holds | Wiki page |
|---|---|---|
| **Activity** | **Today** and **History** | [Activity](./Activity.md) |
| **Now (live)** | **Live** and **Programs** | [Activity](./Activity.md) |
| **Freezes and stutters** | **All events**, and each report | [Freeze reports](./Freeze-reports.md) |
| **Health** | An overview and six areas | [Health](./Health.md) |
| **Disks** | Each disk's alerts and tests in one place | [Health](./Health.md), [Tests](./Tests.md) |
| **Memory** | The memory's alerts and tests | [Health](./Health.md), [Tests](./Tests.md) |
| **Tests** | The memory and each disk | [Tests](./Tests.md) |
| **Optimizations** | Windows settings to change, by list | [Optimizations](./Optimizations.md) |
| **Dumps** | Blue screens, kernel reports and apps that crashed | [Dumps](./Dumps.md) |
| **Captures** | New and past latency captures | [Captures](./Captures.md) |

**Settings** opens from its card on Home, or from the search. See [Settings](./Settings.md).

## Inside a group

A pane on the left holds **Home**, the search box and the pages of the current group. Its bottom line says whether Vestigium is monitoring. A **Related** list of links sits beside the page, or below it in a narrow window.

## Banners

A banner at the top of the page shows when monitoring is not running normally:

- **Monitoring paused.** with **Resume**.
- **Vestigium is not running.** with **Start Vestigium**. The history is still there; nothing new is logged.
- **No connection to the running Vestigium.** with **Try again**. If the monitor is a different version from the window, exit it from the tray and open Vestigium again.

## Detail level, language and colours

- **Detail level**: **Simple**, **Detailed** or **Technical**, for every screen.
- **Language**: English or Portuguese (Brazil), or like Windows.
- **Color mode**: like Windows, **Light** or **Dark**.
- **Color theme**: **Red** (the default), **Graphite**, **Slate** or **Indigo**.

All four live in [Settings](./Settings.md).

## Charts

Point at a chart to read the values at that moment. **Ctrl** and the mouse wheel zoom in and out, and dragging moves along the time. With the keyboard, the arrow keys move, **+** and **−** zoom, **0** shows the whole range and **Esc** clears the reading.
